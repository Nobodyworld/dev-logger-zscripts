# Code of Conduct

## Purpose
The Zscripts community is committed to providing a friendly, safe, and inclusive experience for everyone. This Code of Conduct outlines expectations for all contributors and maintainers when interacting in repositories, issue trackers, discussions, and community spaces.

## Our Standards
- Be respectful, considerate, and collaborative.
- Assume positive intent and offer constructive feedback.
- Welcome diverse perspectives; seek to understand before responding.
- Use inclusive language and avoid personal attacks, harassment, or discrimination.
- Respect differing skill levels and experience; offer help generously.

## Unacceptable Behavior
Examples of unacceptable behavior include:
- Harassment, intimidation, or discrimination in any form.
- Offensive comments, trolling, or deliberate disruption.
- Publishing others' private information without explicit permission.
- Sustained or repeated criticism that veers into personal attacks.

## Responsibilities & Enforcement
- Maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing this Code of Conduct.
- Maintainers may take appropriate corrective action, including warnings, temporary bans, or permanent exclusion from the project.
- Reports of unacceptable behavior should be directed to the maintainers at `security@zscripts.dev`.

## Scope
This Code applies within project spaces and when an individual is representing the project or community in public.

## Reporting & Response
- Email `security@zscripts.dev` with details of the incident, including dates, participants, and any relevant context.
- Maintainers will acknowledge receipt within 2 business days and aim for resolution within 7 business days.
- All reports are handled confidentially and shared only with those directly involved in the investigation.
