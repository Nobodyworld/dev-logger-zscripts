# Code of Conduct

## Purpose

The Zscripts community is committed to providing a friendly, safe, and inclusive
experience for everyone. This Code of Conduct outlines expectations for
contributors and maintainers when interacting in repositories, issue trackers,
discussions, and related community spaces.

## Our Standards

- Be respectful, considerate, and collaborative.
- Assume positive intent and offer constructive feedback.
- Welcome diverse perspectives and seek to understand before responding.
- Use inclusive language and avoid personal attacks, harassment, or
  discrimination.
- Respect differing skill levels and experience.

## Unacceptable Behavior

Examples include:

- Harassment, intimidation, or discrimination in any form.
- Offensive comments, trolling, or deliberate disruption.
- Publishing another person's private information without explicit permission.
- Sustained or repeated criticism that becomes a personal attack.

## Responsibilities and Enforcement

Maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing this Code of Conduct and
may take proportionate corrective action, including warnings, content removal,
temporary restrictions, or permanent exclusion from project spaces.

## Scope

This Code applies within project spaces and when an individual is representing
the project or community in public.

## Reporting

Do not post sensitive incident details publicly.

To request a private reporting channel, open a minimal repository issue titled
`Code of Conduct contact request` without including names, evidence, or incident
details. The maintainer will arrange an appropriate private channel. For conduct
that violates GitHub's platform policies, GitHub's abuse-reporting tools may also
be used directly.

This project does not currently publish a dedicated conduct-reporting mailbox or
a guaranteed response-time service level. Reports will be handled as promptly and
confidentially as practical, with information shared only as needed to evaluate
and address the matter.
